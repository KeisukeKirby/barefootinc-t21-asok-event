Your feet have 26 bones, 33 joints and more than 100 muscles.
Most shoes let almost none of them work.

Barefoot shoes get out of the way. A toe box wide enough for your toes to spread, a sole thin enough to read the ground under you, and no raised heel tipping you forward — so your foot can move, grip and balance the way it was built to. For 20 days at Terminal 21 Asok, put a pair on and feel the difference in a minute.

Vibram FiveFingers

Five separate toes, and nothing between you and the ground.

Every toe gets its own pocket, so your foot can spread, grip and balance the way it does barefoot. The Vibram rubber sole is thin enough to read the surface under you and tough enough for real ground. Models for the gym, the trail, the water and the street.
